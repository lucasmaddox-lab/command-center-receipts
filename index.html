Command Center Receipts

A private, single-user tool that files Luke Maddox's own emailed receipts from his Gmail into his own Google Drive folders. It runs only on his computer.

Privacy policy

This app reads only the Gmail account of its sole user and writes files only to that user's Google Drive. No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or sent to any third party or server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: lucasmaddox@gmail.com.
